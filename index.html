A dedicated, single-window workspace for Meta-Ethnography.

Meta-Ethnography Canvas is a free, browser-based tool built to help qualitative researchers read primary studies, separate 1st and 2nd order concepts, build cross-study translation matrices, and export publication-ready tables—without losing the link to the original PDF pages.

Why This Tool Was Built

Bridging the gap between PDF coding and spreadsheet matrices

When reading published meta-ethnographies in healthcare, a common pattern stands out in the Methods sections: most research teams code their PDFs in general qualitative software (such as NVivo) and then copy and paste their findings into massive Excel spreadsheets to compare studies across rows and columns.

While this workflow gets the job done, splitting the process across two separate tools creates real headaches:

  • Blurred boundaries between constructs: In a standard spreadsheet, it is easy to mix up raw participant quotes (1st Order) with the primary authors' interpretations (2nd Order).
  • Lost context and page links: Once quotes are pasted into Excel cells, you can no longer click a quote to see the surrounding paragraph in the original PDF.
  • Hard-to-track decisions: The eMERGe reporting guideline emphasizes transparent reporting of how studies were ordered, compared, and synthesized—something that is difficult to reconstruct from static spreadsheets.

To solve this for my own upcoming meta-ethnography, I carefully studied the eMERGe reporting guideline (France et al., 2019) and built a workspace where the PDF reader, the cross-study synthesis matrix, the audit trail, and the manuscript tables all live in one place.

How It Supports Your Synthesis

Every tab in the workspace corresponds directly to the core phases of a meta-ethnography.

Tab 1 • Phase 2 & 3

1. Extract, Context & Appraisal

Highlight text in the built-in PDF viewer (1 for participant quotes, 2 for author concepts) or add entries manually for scanned PDFs. Auto-fill citation metadata from Page 1, and record critical appraisal (CASP, JBI, MMAT, ETQS, Dixon-Woods, or a custom tool) alongside conceptual richness.

Tab 2 • Phase 4–6

2. Interactive Synthesis Matrix & CERQual

Order studies chronologically or by conceptual richness to set your Index Paper. Drag 2nd order concepts into 3rd order themes, toggle Reciprocal (🤝) or Refutational (⚡) translations per cell, build your Line of Argument model (with a concept relationship map you can save as .svg), and rate confidence across the four GRADE-CERQual domains.

Tab 3 • Transparency

3. Reflexive Audit Trail & Team Merge

Automatically log interpretive decisions (study ordering, theme evolution, CERQual rationales) and record reflexive positionality memos. Working in a team? Load a co-reviewer's .json file to calculate extraction concordance and merge constructs in browser.

Tab 4 • Phase 7

4. Publication-Ready Manuscript Tables

Instantly generate an eMERGe Methods Summary with a 19-item checklist mapping, Table 1 (Study Characteristics), Table 2 (1st/2nd/3rd Order Synthesis), Table 2b (Concept × Study Contribution Grid), Table 3 (GRADE-CERQual SoQF and/or Evidence Profile), and Appendix A (Audit Trail). Copy directly into Word/Docs, or download native .docx, Markdown, and CSV files.

100% Local & Private in Your Browser

No account, cloud upload, or server installation required. All PDFs, highlights, and notes are stored locally inside your browser's IndexedDB. Export and restore lightweight .json project backups anytime.

Suggested wording for your Methods section

Adapt as needed. The app also compiles a fuller eMERGe Methods Summary from your own project data in Tab 4. 

We conducted a meta-ethnography following Noblit and Hare (1988) and reported it according to the eMERGe reporting guidance (France et al., 2019). Study PDFs were read, 1st and 2nd order constructs were extracted, and translations were mapped using Meta-Ethnography Canvas (Version 1.1.0; Jeong, 2026), a browser-based workspace in which all data were stored locally. Confidence in each synthesized finding was assessed with GRADE-CERQual (Lewin et al., 2018). Interpretive decisions and reflexive memos were recorded in the software's audit trail.
